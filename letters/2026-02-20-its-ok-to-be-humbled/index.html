It's OK to Be Humbled

20 February 2026

It’s OK to be humbled every now and then.

It’s not always easy to accept because we’re so used to being the main character in our head. But it’s an important experience even though it feels off and you want to forget about it as soon as possible.

Don’t shake it off. Don’t dismiss it. Internalize it and let it guide you to recognize and to learn from those who are better than you.
