On Motivation and Inspiration

31 January 2026

Don’t believe the lie that these come to you from some external force. You will never find them.

Motivation and inspiration are generated internally when you take action. When you start doing, they kick in, which drives you to do more, and the cycle continues.

If you want to accomplish something, don’t wait for some mystical muse. Take action and the rest will follow.
